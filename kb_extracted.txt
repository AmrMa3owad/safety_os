Policy Variance French Markets 👉Taxonomy Definition A food allergy is an immune system reaction that occurs shortly after eating a certain food. Food allergy can cause a number of symptoms which include, but are not limited to, the following: Rashes Hives Trouble breathing Anaphylactic Shock Note: Most Common Food Allergens: Peanuts, Tree nuts, Milk (Dairy), Eggs, Sesame, Fish, Shellfish, Soy, Wheat, Lupin, Mustard, Celery, Sulfites Keywords: Allergy, Allergen, Allergic, Anaphylaxis, Anaphylactic+Shock, Swelling, Swollen, Rash, Hives A food intolerance (or food sensitivity) occurs when a person has difficulty digesting a particular food. Food intolerance can cause a number of symptoms which include, but are not limited to, the following: Intestinal gas Abdominal pain Diarrhea General Digestive problems Note: Most Common Food Intolerance/Sensitivities: Dairy (Lactose Intolerance), Gluten (Celiac Disease* or Non-Celiac Gluten Sensitivity), Caffeine, Salicylates (natural chemicals found in many food preservatives, fruits, veggies, spices, nuts, and honey), Fructose (simple sugar), Sulfites. *Celiac Disease is technically an autoimmune condition but is often identified as a food intolerance. Minor/Moderate Injury: Definition A reported injury or illness where the impacted party did not visit the hospital or require emergency medical transportation. Qualifying Example of Minor/Moderate Injury Qualifying Justification "Unknowingly, I consumed a salad with sesame seeds, of which I am very allergic. I needed my epi-pen to mitigate the allergic reaction." The Eater’s report suggests that he or she experienced illness/injury related to food received through the platform. However, the injury did not warrant hospitalization or require emergency medical transportation. “I had to call in sick today at work after there was salmon on my eggs benedict. I’m allergic to seafood that’s why I ordered ham!” The Eater discloses that he or she has a seafood allergy and describes feeling ill after eating seafood that was not part of her Eats order.
